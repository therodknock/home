Studio

RODKNOCK was founded on the idea that the people closest to the work should decide how it is done.

How we work

There is no management hierarchy. Each person chooses the project they believe matters most and joins the team building it. Teams form around ideas and dissolve when the work is finished.

Decisions are written down and kept in a shared handbook that every employee can edit. Compensation is set through peer review, and credit is recorded by project rather than by role.

We release when a product is ready. We have never announced a date we did not already know we could meet.

Ownership

Every employee holds equity and a vote on studio direction.

Craft

We would rather ship less and maintain what we ship.

Openness

Internal documents and postmortems are shared with the whole team.
