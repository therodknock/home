Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

Rodknock ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our mobile applications and games (collectively, the "Services") available on the Google Play Store handle user information and data.

1. Zero Personally Identifiable Information (PII) Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or harvest any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or payment details.

Our systems and servers do not store any personal profiles or tracking identifiers.

2. Interaction with Google Data

Our games may interact with your Google Account services (such as Google Play Games Services) strictly to enhance your gameplay experience (e.g., displaying your player name or profile avatar on the screen, tracking local achievements, or saving progress).

  • On-Device Display Only: Any Google user data accessed (such as your display name or profile picture) is processed locally on your device solely to present it to you while playing.
  • No Server Transfers: Your Google account data is never transmitted to external servers, stored in our databases, or shared with third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Our games may utilize standard Google Play SDKs (such as Google Play Games Services or basic Google Play platform utilities) to support core gameplay features. These services function within Google’s secure framework. We encourage you to review Google’s Privacy Policy for more details on how Google handles data.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because our games do not collect personal information or transmit user data to external servers, they are safe for a general audience. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under the age of 13 (or 16 depending on jurisdiction).

5. Security

Since we do not collect or store any personal data on external servers, your personal information is not exposed to risk through our services. Any Google profile data stays protected within your device's local runtime environment and Google’s own secured APIs.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Effective Date."
