Software

The tools we use internally are available to other studios under straightforward licences.

Rodkit Engine

A C++ real-time engine with an entity component system, an immediate-mode editor and a documented serialization format. Source is included with every licence.

Rodkit Net

Authoritative-server networking with lobbies, rollback and prediction, designed to drop into Unity or native projects.

Tally

Collects match telemetry and reports balance trends, retention and performance across builds. Currently in open beta.
