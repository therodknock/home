What we make

RODKNOCK Solutions develops multiplayer games and the real-time software behind them. We write our own engine, our own networking layer and our own editor tools, because owning the stack lets small teams ship with confidence.

We publish what we build directly to players and licence our tools to other studios. We have no investors to answer to, so the decision on what to build next rests with the people building it.
